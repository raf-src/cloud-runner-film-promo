Synopsis

CLOUD RUNNER plunges into a fractured sky- world where a gleaming capital city above the clouds siphons all energy from the darkened floating islands below. When five teenage scavengers—known as the Cloud Runners—unearth an ancient power core capable of relighting their starved home, they become the target of Cassian Vane, the ruthless Regent’s Heir, in a desperate race across the industrial mist before the light is extinguished forever.

Only in cinemas Fall 2027.

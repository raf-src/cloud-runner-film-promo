# Cloud Runner

A promotional website for the original dystopian sci-fi film *Cloud Runner*

"In a world of floating industrial cities, five teenage tech-scavengers known as the Cloud Runners race to reach a legendary generator city to restore power to their region—hunted across the skies by Caspian, ruthless heir to the White City, a colossal spaceship city that roams the regions siphoning energy from powerless floating islands, until his peacekeeping sister, Silver, teams up with the crew's young leader to try and stop him and bring unity between their worlds."

## User Experience (UX)

### Project Goals

The primary goal of the *Cloud Runner* promotional website is to build anticipation ahead of its Fall 2027 cinema release by immersing visitors in the film's fractured sky-world, introducing its core characters and lore, previewing official teaser footage, and capturing audience interest through an interactive early-access sign-up form.

#### User Goals

Visitors to the site fall into three main audience groups:

1. **Sci-Fi & Dystopian Film Fans (Primary Audience):**
   * Experience the visual atmosphere and tone of *Cloud Runner* immediately upon landing via the full-viewport hero video and teaser banner.
   * Understand the core premise, world lore, and release timeline through the synopsis and poster showcase.
   * Explore key scenes and character profiles (Rowan, the Cloud Runners, Caspian Vane, and Silver) in the interactive gallery.

2. **Prospective Viewers & Newsletter Subscribers:**
   * Sign up for release alerts, trailer drops, and early-access updates using a clean, accessible subscription form (`updates.html`).
   * Select specific content preferences (such as trailers, behind-the-scenes lore, or release dates) and receive immediate confirmation feedback.

3. **Press, Reviewers & Industry Visitors:**
   * Quickly locate official cast and production credits (starring cast, director, and screenplay details).
   * Navigate effortlessly across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices without layout breakage.

### User Stories

* **Cinematic Hero Header (#1):** As a site visitor, I want a full-viewport cinematic hero header when I land on the page, so that I am immediately immersed in the dystopian atmosphere of *Cloud Runner* and can access key calls to action.
* **Film Details & Synopsis Grid (#2):** As a film enthusiast, I want to view the official movie poster alongside a clear synopsis and full cast/crew details, so that I can understand the plot and background of the film.
* **Video Teaser & Media Banner (#3):** As an explorer of the site, I want to see a cinematic teaser video section directly below the synopsis, so that I can preview official footage and engage with the visual media.
* **Responsive Multi-Page Navigation (#4):** As a user on any device (mobile or desktop), I want intuitive navigation across all pages (`index.html`, `gallery.html`, `updates.html`), so that I can browse the site quickly without layout breakage.
* **Early-Access Contact & Newsletter Form (#5):** As an engaged visitor, I want to submit my details via a clean early-access sign-up form, so that I can receive release updates and alerts for *Cloud Runner*.
* **Character Gallery (#6):** As a site visitor exploring the *Cloud Runner* gallery page, I want an immersive top hero section followed by horizontal image galleries for film scenes and character portraits, so that I can explore the visual lore and characters without clutter.
